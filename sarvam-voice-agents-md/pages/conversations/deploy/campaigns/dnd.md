# Do Not Disturb (DND)

Source: https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/dnd

The **DND list** — opened from the button at the top-right of the [Campaigns page](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns) — is an org-wide suppression list. Any number on it is **excluded from every outbound campaign** in your org, so you can honour opt-outs across all your campaigns in one place.

Add numbers manually, in bulk, or through the **DND API**. Numbers added via the API also appear in this list.

**Calling compliance is your responsibility.** Make sure you have consent to call each contact and that your list respects Do Not Disturb (DND) and any local telemarketing regulations. Non-compliant dialing can get your numbers blocked and may carry legal risk.
