# Best practices

Source: https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/best-practices

A short checklist for launching campaigns safely and protecting your number health.

[1](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/best-practices#validate-cohorts-before-launch)

### Validate cohorts before launch

Catch bad phone numbers and mismatched columns before they burn through your dial budget. See [Cohorts](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/cohorts).

[2](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/best-practices#start-small)

### Start small

Launch with a test cohort of 50–100 contacts and a low CPS. Review call quality and connect rates before adding the full list.

[3](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/best-practices#pause--edit--resume)

### Pause → Edit → Resume

Never try to change a running campaign. [Pause it first](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/campaign-lifecycle), make changes, then resume.

[4](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/best-practices#use-groups-for-number-rotation)

### Use groups for number rotation

Assign a [group](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/telephony/groups) so the campaign cycles through numbers and protects number health.

[5](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/deploy/campaigns/best-practices#watch-the-first-hour)

### Watch the first hour

Monitor [Connectivity](https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/monitor/agent-analytics/connectivity) right after launch. If connect rates drop sharply, pause and investigate.
