# Announcing Voice Agents

Source: https://docs.sarvam.ai/conversations/newly-launched

**Voice Agents is here: the end-to-end stack for building voice agents that actually work in India, now available to everyone.**

Formerly known as **Samvaad**, the product is now called Voice Agents.

For a while, you couldn’t sign up directly for Voice Agents. It ran in production alongside enterprises, shipped by our forward-deployed engineers on real calls, at scale. Every hard problem a voice agent hits in the wild, we hit first: messy telephony lines, code-mixed speech, callers who interrupt, languages that switch mid-sentence, and flows that have to hold up across thousands of concurrent calls.

That taught us what a voice-agent stack really needs to get right.

## Why we’re opening it up

Over the last few months, something kept happening. More and more teams came to us wanting to build voice agents, and the same gap kept showing up: there was no platform that solved this well for Indian businesses and developers who weren’t at enterprise scale but needed a quick, reliable way to get to production without writing a bunch of code. The tooling that existed assumed you had a team of engineers to wire up UIs, stitch together models, and babysit orchestration code.

So we spent the last few months turning what we had built into something ready for you.

## What Voice Agents is

An **opinionated, end-to-end stack** that orchestrates a **constellation of models** (speech recognition, reasoning, and speech synthesis) into one coherent voice agent. We made the hard choices so you don’t have to, and as the paradigms for orchestrating these models have shifted, the stack has moved with them, so your agents keep improving as the models underneath them do.

It all lives in a **single workspace**. One place to build, test, manage, deploy, run campaigns, and read the data back, with no UIs to wire and no orchestration code to write.

Build & test

Design agents, then test them in test agent before going live.

Manage

Keep agents, versions, and settings organised as your setup grows.

Deploy

Go live on telephony: bring your own provider or rent a number from Sarvam.

Run campaigns

Kick off batch outbound calling at scale.

See the data

Call logs, analytics, and custom dashboards over every conversation.

Improve

Fetch insights from your conversations and keep improving your agents.

Opinionated doesn’t mean rigid. You still control the prompt, the voice, the tools, and the flow. You just don’t have to build the plumbing underneath them. From a blank agent to a live phone call, everything you need is already here.
